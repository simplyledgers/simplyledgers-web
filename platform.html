The SimplyLedgers Platform

One workspace from source documents to compliant workings

SimplyLedgers is an AI-powered accounting automation platform for CA firms and finance teams. It reads your invoices and bank statements, maps them to the right ledgers, prepares GST and TDS workings, and syncs reviewed entries with TallyPrime.

02AI Document Intelligence

Every document read, classified and tracked

Upload invoices and bank statements into one Document Inbox. SimplyLedgers reads each document, recognises what it is and moves it through processing, so your team always knows what has been processed, what needs review and what needs a retry.

  • One inbox for all source documents, per company and period
  • Clear status for every document: processed, pending review or failed
  • Daily processing overview across the selected company
03Invoice Processing & Data Extraction

Accounting-ready data from every invoice

SimplyLedgers extracts the details your accounting and GST work depends on, straight from the invoice, and keeps them structured for the steps that follow — no retyping from PDFs.

  • Vendor, invoice and tax details captured in a structured format
  • Extracted data flows directly into ledger mapping and GST workings
  • Invoices that need attention are routed to review

Purchase invoice

Uploaded to Document Inbox

Fields captured

  • Vendor name & GSTIN
  • Invoice number & date
  • Line items
  • Taxable value
  • CGST · SGST · IGST
  • Invoice total
04Bank Statement Processing

Bank statements turned into reviewable entries

Upload bank statements alongside your invoices. Transactions are read line by line and prepared as entries against the right ledgers, ready for your team to check.

  • Statements uploaded per company from the same workspace
  • Transactions captured with their date, narration and amount
  • Suggested ledgers for each entry, confirmed by your team

Bank statement workflow

  1. Upload bank statements

    Add statements for the selected company and period.

  2. Transactions read

    Each entry is captured with its date, narration and amount.

  3. Ledgers suggested

    Entries are matched to the right ledgers for review.

  4. Ready to post

    Approved entries move on to TallyPrime.

05AI Ledger Mapping

The right ledger for every entry

SimplyLedgers suggests the party, expense and tax ledgers for each document based on your chart of accounts. What your team confirms is remembered, so mapping gets faster with every period.

  • Ledger suggestions based on your own TallyPrime ledgers
  • Unmapped vendors highlighted as pending actions
  • Confirmed mappings saved to the Vendor Master for reuse

How mapping works

  • Vendor on the documentParty ledger
  • Nature of expenseExpense ledger
  • Tax on the invoiceGST input ledgers

Unmapped vendors are flagged for your team. Confirmed mappings are kept in the Vendor Master and reused next time.

06TallyPrime Two-Way Synchronization

Works with the books you already keep

SimplyLedgers connects to TallyPrime in both directions: it uses your existing ledgers and masters for mapping, and posts approved entries back to your books. Zoho Books support is coming soon.

  • Ledgers and masters brought in from TallyPrime
  • Reviewed entries posted to TallyPrime in one step
  • A clear record of what has been posted to Tally

07GST Workings & GSTR-2B Reconciliation

GST workings and GSTR-2B matching in one view

Prepare GST workings from processed purchase data and reconcile your books against GSTR-2B. Every invoice is placed in a clear category, so exceptions are easy to find and resolve.

  • Exact and probable matches between books and GSTR-2B
  • Value mismatches surfaced for correction
  • Invoices missing in GSTR-2B or missing in books listed separately
08TDS Workings & Calculations

TDS workings prepared from your processed data

SimplyLedgers uses the invoices it has already processed to identify TDS-applicable expenses and prepare section-wise workings, so your team reviews calculations instead of building them.

  • TDS-applicable expenses identified from processed invoices
  • Section-wise workings for each vendor
  • Workings reviewed and finalised by your team

TDS workings

  1. Applicable expenses identified

    Processed invoices are checked for TDS applicability.

  2. Section & rate applied

    Deductions are worked out section-wise for each vendor.

  3. Workings reviewed

    Your team checks and adjusts before finalising.

  4. Ready for compliance

    Workings are available for export with your records.

09Review, Approval & Accounting Exports

Your team stays in control of every entry

Automation prepares the work; people approve it. Items that need attention are collected as pending actions, and approved data is available for posting and export.

  • Invoices requiring review and unmapped vendors in one list
  • Approve before anything is posted to your books
  • Reports and exports for your records and filings
10Security, Data Protection & Audit Trail

Built for work that has to stand up to scrutiny

Client data is handled with the care accounting work requires. Work is organised by company, changes go through review, and a dedicated audit trail keeps a record of what happened and when.

  • Work organised by company and financial year
  • Human approval before entries are posted
  • Audit trail of uploads, edits, approvals and postings

Organised by client company

Each client company and financial year is selected and worked on separately.

Review before posting

Nothing reaches your books without a person approving it.

Audit trail

Uploads, edits, approvals and postings stay on record.
